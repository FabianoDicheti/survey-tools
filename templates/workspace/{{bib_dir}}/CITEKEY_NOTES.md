## One work, one key

When one work has two DOIs (a conference and a journal version, or a reprint), keep one key and
record the other as an `ALT-DOI:` segment in the ledger reason so the candidate merge catches it.
When a reviewer's "2024" is a reprint of a 2020 paper, cite the version read and record both.
