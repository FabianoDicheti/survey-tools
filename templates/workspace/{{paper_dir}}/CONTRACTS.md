## Rules binding on every writer

1. Every citekey is in `refs.bib` **and** used in some synthesis. Method-section exceptions: works
   analysed in `prior_surveys.md`, and `{{out}}` rows cited only to state scope.
2. Never re-argue a neighbouring section; cite it by §x.y.
3. The crosscut section introduces no evidence; every paragraph carries a §x.y back-reference.
4. Class rules from `CITATION_CLASSES.md`; numbers are always someone else's, attributed in the sentence.
5. Headline counts are `{{n:<key>}}` macros from `survey stats`, never typed.
6. Typed placeholders for every hole; no internal file names or tool names in prose.
7. Third person; take positions; say when evidence is thin.
8. Raise the strongest objection to your own section yourself; a defence that is a hypothesis is
   labelled one. Concede staleness as well as singularity of a key measurement, everywhere it repeats.
